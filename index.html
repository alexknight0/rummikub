Rules
  • Sets are groups (3–4 tiles, same number, different colours) or runs (3+ tiles in a row, same colour). 1 can't follow 13.
  • Your first play must be worth at least 30 points, using only tiles from your rack.
  • After that you can rearrange anything on the table, as long as every set is valid when you finish.
  • Tiles already on the table can't go back to your rack.
  • If you can't (or don't want to) play, draw a tile. First to empty their rack wins.
  • A joker stands in for any tile. Left in your rack at the end it costs 30.
